How I Sell
The operating principles behind the numbers
Discovery
I listen to understand — not to respond. Every discovery call starts with questions about what they want to know, what the intent is, and what the preferred outcome looks like. Better questions reveal root causes that surface-level pitches miss.
Relationship
I prepare before and debrief after every client conversation. Peers describe me as someone who follows through on every commitment — every time. In enterprise sales, trust is the close.
Multi-Threading
I map buying committees deliberately — identifying economic buyers, champions, and blockers early. I build at multiple levels so no single contact change stalls a deal in the final mile.
Pipeline Discipline
I treat my territory like a business — owning forecast accuracy, managing the full funnel, and never letting the deals in flight distract from building what comes next.
Under Pressure
More precise, not more reactive. Mission-oriented. I compete against standards — not people — and that steadiness is what clients feel at the end of a deal when things get complicated.
Conviction
I put my heart and mind into what I sell. The clients who've worked with me longest describe it as genuine — not transactional. That's not a soft skill. It's a revenue driver.
