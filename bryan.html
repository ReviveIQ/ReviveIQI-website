Operating Principles
What I actually believe
01
Communication can solve 80% of any issue. But miscommunication will cause 100% of your avoidable problems. Most pipeline stalls aren't deal problems — they're communication failures that compounded.
02
Quality conversations require quality questions. Before asking anything: What do you actually want to know? What's the intent? What's your preferred outcome? Better questions reveal root causes that surface-level diagnostics miss.
03
Put your heart and mind into what you do. People enjoy what they invest in. I show up fully — to every conversation, every diagnosis, every team I lead — and that energy is contagious in the right environment.
