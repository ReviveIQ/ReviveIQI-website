Operating Philosophy
What I actually believe about work, leadership, and people
These aren't values on a poster. They're principles I operate from every day — in every conversation, every diagnosis, every system I build.
01
Communication can solve 80% of any issue. But miscommunication will cause 100% of your avoidable problems. Most pipeline stalls aren't deal problems — they're communication failures that compounded over time.
02
Quality conversations start with quality questions. Before solving anything, I want to know: What do we actually want to know? Why are we asking? What's the intended outcome? Better questions reveal the root causes that surface-level diagnostics miss.
03
People enjoy what they invest in. Buy-in isn't optional — it's the operating condition for sustained performance. I build systems people actually use because they were part of building them.
Working With Me
Who you're actually hiring when you work with me
Synthesized from DiSC, Myers-Briggs, Predictive Index, Thomas-Kilmann, PeopleTek 360, Learning to Listen, and Criteria Workplace assessments — validated across multiple frameworks and confirmed by peer and manager feedback.
💬
Communication Style
Direct, factual, and substantive. I communicate best in writing for complex decisions and prefer context and stated intent up front. In client conversations, I listen to understand before responding — confirmed by peers as a genuine strength. When I'm unclear on something, I'll ask a precise question rather than assume. I do not do small talk for its own sake, but I warm up quickly in substantive conversation. If information is unclear, I may speak at length — I actively work to stay concise.
⚙️
Decision Making
Deliberate before committing, confident once decided. I gather facts, analyze systematically, and separate emotion from conclusion. I move quickly when data is sufficient and slow down when it isn't — and I'm transparent about which mode I'm in. I set high standards and notice when they're not met — in my own work first, then in others'. I don't reverse decisions without cause and don't expect others to either.
🤝
Collaboration
Strongly independent by default, but genuinely collaborative when the work calls for it. I'm described by peers as someone who prepares before and reflects after every client call, who mentors when asked, and who develops others consistently. I share expertise readily and give direct feedback without it being personal. I work best when accountability exists across the team — not just on my end.
🔥
Under Pressure
I become more precise, not more reactive. My default conflict mode is Avoiding (65th percentile) — I don't wade into friction unnecessarily, but I will engage directly when the issue matters. My initial response to constructive criticism can be defensive — I know this and address it by letting the initial reaction pass before engaging. Once I'm in, I'm in to solve, not to win.
✨
What Brings Out My Best
Clear expectations, autonomy to execute, and a team that holds itself accountable. I thrive on novel problems, creative environments, and roles that evolve — I become unmotivated when stuck in repetition. I perform best when encouraged and collaborating (peer feedback), when the goals are measurable, and when the work requires both analytical rigor and human judgment. Recognition for results matters more to me than recognition for effort.
📊
Motivators & Energy
Solving complex problems through logic. Creating efficient systems. Setting and meeting high standards. Proving out an idea with data before presenting it. Independent, deep-focus work. Mission-oriented environments where loyalty and follow-through are the baseline. I get competitive by nature — I track performance against a standard, not against other people — and I bring that energy into every engagement.
