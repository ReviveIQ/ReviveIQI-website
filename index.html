ResumeIQ Exclusive

The section no other
resume tool offers.

Every resume in the pile tells hiring managers what a candidate did. None of them answer the question every hiring manager is actually asking: how does this person work?

The "Working With Me" section changes that. It translates your DISC, MBTI, Predictive Index, or TKI results into professional behavioral language — your communication style, how you make decisions, how you collaborate, and what brings out your best work. Written in the language hiring managers actually use, not assessment jargon.

Culture fit is one of the top reasons strong candidates get rejected at the final stage — and it's assessed almost entirely on gut feel because candidates never give hiring managers anything to evaluate it on. Working With Me changes the conversation from a guess to a data point.
